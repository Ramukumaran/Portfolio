Titanic Survival Analysis

Python · Pandas · Seaborn
Dataset
891 passengers, 12 columns (CSV)
Tools
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
Output
8 charts and a cleaned dataset

Problem

Which passengers were most likely to survive the Titanic, and what do age, class, gender and fare tell us?

Data

Passenger records with survival, class, gender, age, family size, fare and port of embarkation. Age was missing for 177 passengers, Cabin for 687, and Embarked for 2.

Method

Loaded and inspected the data with Pandas. Filled missing ages with the mean and missing ports with the most common value, dropped the mostly empty Cabin column, and checked for duplicates (none found). Then grouped and summarised the data and built 8 charts with Matplotlib and Seaborn.

Key insights

  • Only 38.4% of passengers survived (342 of 891).
  • Gender was the biggest gap: 74.2% of women survived against 18.9% of men.
  • Survival fell with class: 63% in 1st, 47% in 2nd and 24% in 3rd.
  • 1st class paid an average fare of about 84, against about 14 in 3rd class.

World Cup Match Analysis in SQL

MySQL · CTEs · Window functions
Dataset
world_cup_games table, 1,672 rows
Tools
MySQL, Jupyter Notebook
Work done
16 groups of queries

Problem

Answer football questions straight from a database: which teams score the most, and how do they rank?

Data

One row per team per match, with attendance, stage, year, stadium, referee and goals.

Method

Connected MySQL to a Jupyter notebook and wrote queries from simple SELECTs up to aggregations, subqueries, CTEs and window functions.

Key insights

  • Brazil ranks first with 220 World Cup goals in this dataset.
  • Argentina and FRG tie for second with 131 goals each.
  • Italy (128), France (106), Germany (93), Spain (91) and the Netherlands (87) complete the top eight.

Walmart Global Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

Power BI · Excel
Period
2011 to 2014
Tools
Power BI, Excel
Interactive
Year range and country filters

Problem

Give a business team one page to see sales, profit and discounts by category, customer segment and country.

Method

Built KPI cards, a segment and category bar chart, a profit donut, a year-over-year line chart and a colour-coded country by category heatmap, with filters for year range and country.

Key insights

  • The Consumer segment brings 52.06% of profit, then Corporate (32.71%) and Home Office (15.24%).
  • Consumer leads sales in every category: Technology 0.47M, Office Supplies 0.43M, Furniture 0.34M.
  • Headline numbers: about 2M total sales, 283K total profit and an 11.0% average discount.

Job Salary Dashboard (Excel)

Excel · Pivot Tables · Slicers
Dataset
250,000 job records
Tools
Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers
Filters

Problem

Compare average pay across tech roles and see how jobs are spread across industries.

Method
